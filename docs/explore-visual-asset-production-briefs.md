# ILND Keşfet — özgün görsel üretim brief'leri

Mevcut kapaklar yeni asset'ler üretilene kadar korunur. Her brief ayrı bir visual family kullanır; ortak nokta ILND'nin sakin, doğal ve editoryal dünyasıdır.

## 1. Ozempic: sayıların söylediği ve söylemediği

**CONTENT**  
Ozempic: sayıların söylediği ve söylemediği

**VISUAL CONCEPT**  
Markasız bir ilaç kalemi formu, klinik araştırma notları ve ölçüm izleriyle kurulmuş tarafsız bir araştırma masası. İlaç nesnesi konuyu çağrıştırır; kompozisyon bir reklam değil, veriyi inceleyen editoryal bir çalışma hissi verir.

**IMAGE GENERATION PROMPT**  
Create a single premium editorial still-life photograph for a wellness magazine article about interpreting clinical evidence around a weight-management medication. Show an unbranded white medical injection pen-like object beside anonymous printed data sheets with abstract bars and dots, a graphite pencil, and a small glass of water on a pale stone research table. No readable text, no logos, no recognizable packaging. The image should feel observational and journalistic, with tactile paper and stone textures, restrained styling, and a clear subject hierarchy. No people.

**NEGATIVE / AVOID**  
Real medication branding, recognizable Ozempic packaging, readable copy, syringes with needles, blood, hospital drama, doctor or patient portrait, generic medical stock, glossy pharmaceutical advertising, neon blue, oversaturated color, watermark.

**COMPOSITION**  
Top-down three-quarter still life. Medical object in the foreground, data sheets offset diagonally, generous quiet negative space for card cropping. No collage and no infographic text.

**LIGHTING**  
Soft north-window daylight, gentle directional shadow, neutral exposure, subtle paper texture.

**COLOR DIRECTION**  
Warm cream, pale stone, charcoal graphite and a restrained ILND sage accent.

**ASPECT RATIO**  
3:2 landscape, safe for a square crop.

**FILENAME**  
`ozempic-klinik-veri.webp`

## 2. Sporcu beslenmesi

**CONTENT**  
Sporcu beslenmesi: üç sayı, bir de yıkılmış efsane

**VISUAL CONCEPT**  
Dengeli gerçek bir öğünü, porsiyon ve makro fikrini mutfak masasında gösteren food-editorial sahne. İnsan yok; yiyecekler konunun kendisi.

**IMAGE GENERATION PROMPT**  
Create a natural editorial food photograph of a balanced post-training meal: grilled salmon or beans for protein, roasted grains or potatoes for carbohydrate, and varied green vegetables, arranged as one honest plate with a small separate bowl of ingredients. Include a simple kitchen scale and an unmarked measuring spoon at the edge of frame to suggest portions without showing numbers or text. Real food texture, imperfect but appetizing plating, premium food magazine styling, no person, no supplement containers.

**NEGATIVE / AVOID**  
Fitness model, bodybuilder, protein powder tub, shaker advertisement, dramatic gym lighting, sterile meal-prep grid, artificial plastic food, readable labels, logo, excessive saturation.

**COMPOSITION**  
Eye-level to table-height close shot. One hero plate, ingredients creating a natural diagonal, scale secondary. Keep edges calm for card crop.

**LIGHTING**  
Late-morning side light with soft falloff and believable shadows.

**COLOR DIRECTION**  
Leaf green, roasted ochre, salmon coral, cream ceramic and muted wood.

**ASPECT RATIO**  
3:2 landscape, safe for a square crop.

**FILENAME**  
`sporcu-beslenmesi-dengeli-tabak.webp`

## 3. Sporun güne etkisi

**CONTENT**  
Sporun güne etkisi

**VISUAL CONCEPT**  
Açık havada yürüyüşün/kolay koşunun bedensel ritmini ve sonrasındaki zihinsel açıklığı gösteren geniş manzara. İnsan yalnızca ölçekte ve hareket çizgisinde var.

**IMAGE GENERATION PROMPT**  
Create a cinematic but natural editorial photograph of an adult person walking at an easy pace on a coastal Mediterranean path in clear morning daylight. Show the person from behind or in partial silhouette, face not visible, with one foot in motion and a long open horizon ahead. The landscape, moving air and changing light should suggest mental clarity after movement. Authentic clothing and posture, documentary calm, no staged fitness pose, no equipment branding.

**NEGATIVE / AVOID**  
Gym, treadmill, influencer pose, close-up face, running race, sportswear campaign, neon colors, exaggerated speed effects, city stock photo, logo, watermark.

**COMPOSITION**  
Wide environmental frame with a diagonal path leading toward the horizon. Person occupies less than one third of the image; leave breathing room around the sky and landscape.

**LIGHTING**  
Fresh low-angle morning sun, natural highlights, soft atmospheric depth.

**COLOR DIRECTION**  
Sea blue-green, olive foliage, warm sand, washed sky and cream neutrals.

**ASPECT RATIO**  
3:2 landscape, safe for a square crop.

**FILENAME**  
`sporun-gune-etkisi-acik-hava.webp`

## 4. Bağırsak diyetleri

**CONTENT**  
Bağırsak diyetleri

**VISUAL CONCEPT**  
Lifli yiyecekleri ve mikrobiyom fikrini korkutmadan anlatan food + bilimsel çizgi birleşimi. İllüstrasyon yalnızca küçük, soyut bir destek olarak kullanılır.

**IMAGE GENERATION PROMPT**  
Create a refined editorial still life showing legumes, oats, lentils, leafy greens, onions and colorful vegetables on a linen-covered table. In the far background, include a very subtle hand-drawn scientific line motif inspired by a healthy microbiome, abstract and non-literal, like a quiet magazine annotation with no words. Food remains the clear hero. Tactile, trustworthy, contemporary nutrition editorial, no people.

**NEGATIVE / AVOID**  
Anatomical intestines, medical fear imagery, bacteria close-ups, clinical blue hospital setting, doctor portrait, digestive tract diagram with labels, gross-out textures, logos, watermark.

**COMPOSITION**  
Three-quarter tabletop still life. Whole ingredients in the foreground, one open grain bowl as anchor, abstract scientific motif softly receding behind them.

**LIGHTING**  
Diffuse daylight, gentle shadow under ingredients, no hard flash.

**COLOR DIRECTION**  
Natural greens, lentil brown, oat cream, beetroot plum and muted sage.

**ASPECT RATIO**  
3:2 landscape, safe for a square crop.

**FILENAME**  
`bagirsak-diyetleri-lifli-gidalar.webp`

## 5. Sporcu içeceği rehberi

**CONTENT**  
Sporcu içeceği rehberi

**VISUAL CONCEPT**  
İçeceğin kendisini ve elektrolit çağrışımını temiz bir stille anlatan hydration still-life. İnsan tamamen dışarıda; spor reklamı yerine editoryal ürün fotoğrafı.

**IMAGE GENERATION PROMPT**  
Create a clean premium editorial still-life of a matte reusable sports bottle with condensation droplets, a clear glass of water, lemon and orange wedges, and a tiny unbranded ceramic pinch bowl of sea salt. Place everything on pale stone with a folded sage linen towel. The drink and ingredients should look real and refreshing, with no commercial product identity. Quiet, tactile, natural hydration story for a wellness magazine.

**NEGATIVE / AVOID**  
Energy drink can, commercial bottle branding, athlete, gym, neon blue, explosive splash, artificial CGI shine, readable text, logo, watermark, oversaturated citrus.

**COMPOSITION**  
Close three-quarter still life. Bottle as hero on one side, citrus and water balancing the frame, salt bowl small and understated. Clear negative space for crop.

**LIGHTING**  
Cool-clean morning light with soft specular highlights on droplets and glass.

**COLOR DIRECTION**  
Cream stone, translucent water, restrained citrus yellow, sage green and charcoal accents.

**ASPECT RATIO**  
3:2 landscape, safe for a square crop.

**FILENAME**  
`sporcu-icecegi-hidrasyon-still-life.webp`
